Principles

What we hold to.

Started by people who wanted integrated automation in their own homes and could not find it done credibly in India. Four ideas have held since.

01234
  1. 01

    Technical depth

    Continuous innovation, backed by our own investment in technical research, so the systems we design are ones we understand completely.

  2. 02

    Service, first

    Easy to install, easy to use and easy to do business with. If any one of the three fails, the job isn’t finished.

  3. 03

    Holistic, not piecemeal

    Not a point solution bolted on, but a fundamental change to the comfort, convenience, security and safety of the whole home.

  4. 04

    Credible partners

    Alliances with world-class product and technology providers, so what goes into the walls is something we would live with ourselves.

Teams

Five cities.
One standard.

Edomotics began in 2011 with people who wanted integrated automation in their own homes and couldn’t find a credible way to get it in India. So they built the practice instead. Today it is a team of more than sixty, based in Hyderabad and Bengaluru, with projects across Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Vijayawada and Visakhapatnam.
