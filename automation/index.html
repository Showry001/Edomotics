Scenes

Scenes,
not switches.

Every room keeps a few moods. One press sets the light, curtains, climate and music together, so the house is ready before you are.

Drag, swipe or use the arrows

  • Arrive

    The hall and living room come up before you reach the door.

  • Dinner

    Pendants over the table. Everything else held low.

  • Unwind

    Cove light only. Curtains drawn. Music, quietly, in the next room too.

  • Read

    One warm pendant, close. The rest of the room asleep.

  • Sleep

    A single press at the bedside, and the whole house goes dark.

  • Wake

    Curtains open slowly, at the pace of the light outside.
