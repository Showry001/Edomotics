Our story

Edomotics was started by people who wanted integrated automation in their own homes and could not find high-quality, credible brands in the Indian market. So they built the practice instead.

The aim has stayed the same since 2011: to be innovative, passionate and nimble, to delight the people we work for, and to deliver a better way of living at home.

Today Edomotics designs integrated automation and audio-video systems for residential and commercial spaces, with a team of more than sixty based in Hyderabad and Bengaluru, and projects across Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Vijayawada and Visakhapatnam.

Principles

What we hold to.

Four ideas have shaped every project since the first.

01234
  1. 01

    Technical depth

    Continuous innovation, backed by our own investment in technical research, so the systems we design are ones we understand completely.

  2. 02

    Service, first

    Easy to install, easy to use and easy to do business with. If any one of the three fails, the job isn’t finished.

  3. 03

    Holistic, not piecemeal

    Not a point solution bolted on, but a fundamental change to the comfort, convenience, security and safety of the whole home.

  4. 04

    Credible partners

    Alliances with world-class product and technology providers, so what goes into the walls is something we would live with ourselves.
